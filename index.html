Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

WHOOP Personal Analyzer is a personal application created for accessing and analyzing the developer's own WHOOP health and fitness data.

Data Access

The application may access WHOOP data including recovery, sleep, workouts, cycles, profile information, and body measurements after authorization through WHOOP OAuth.

Use of Data

WHOOP data accessed by this application is used solely for personal analysis and visualization.

Data Sharing

Personal WHOOP data is not sold or shared with third parties.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect authentication credentials and personal data used by the application.

Contact

For questions regarding this privacy policy, please contact the application owner through the contact information provided to WHOOP.
